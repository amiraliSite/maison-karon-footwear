### Architectural Highlights
- **Obsidian Neon & Frosted Glassmorphism UI** — `#06080F` luminous canvas with ambient cyan/indigo glow orbs and translucent glass cards.
- **Interactive Atelier Tree Diagram (`نمودار درختی`)** — Explore product architecture and construction lineage via glowing SVG Bezier branches.
- **Luminous Sliders & Archive Runway** — Smooth animated hero campaign slider, snap horizontal runway carousel, and editorial lookbook plates.
- **Authentication & Member Pass** — Built-in Login & Registration system (`ورود / عضویت`) with persistent member profile state.
- **Full Trilingual & Bi-Directional (`LTR` + `RTL`) Support** — Seamless live switching between English (`EN`), Persian (`FA`), and French (`FR`).
- **End-to-End Shopping Bag & Checkout** — EU sizing matrix, live stock status, slide-over glassmorphic bag drawer, and order dispatch flow.
